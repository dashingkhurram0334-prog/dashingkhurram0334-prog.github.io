NICOP Fees for Overseas Pakistanis

LAST CHECKED · 2026

NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis) has its own fee structure, separate from the domestic Smart CNIC. Charges are billed in USD and vary slightly depending on which zone you're applying from.

Standard NICOP Fees

ProcessingFee (USD)Timeline
Normal$40≈ 20-30 working days
Urgent$70≈ 10-15 working days
Executive$90≈ 5-7 working days

Renewal / Modification

Renewing an existing NICOP or modifying details generally follows the same fee tiers as a new application, though some Pakistani missions abroad set slightly different local rates in the equivalent local currency.

Note: NICOP fees are set per diplomatic mission and can vary by country. Always confirm the exact amount with your nearest Pakistani Embassy, Consulate, or the official Pak-Identity portal before applying.
