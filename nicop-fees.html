NICOP Fees 2026 — Complete Guide

ZONE A & ZONE B RATES 2026

NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis) is for Pakistani citizens living, working, or studying abroad. Unlike a domestic CNIC, NICOP is issued specifically to overseas Pakistanis and carries different fees depending on which region you're applying from. This guide breaks down the exact fees, eligibility, and application process for both zones.

Who Should Apply for NICOP?

You need a NICOP if you're a Pakistani citizen currently residing abroad and need an official Pakistani identity document. NICOP is valid for 10 years and can be renewed. It's accepted for travel, banking, property transactions, and other official purposes within Pakistan and internationally.

Documents Required for NICOP

Your application must include: a valid passport or national ID, a recent passport-sized photograph, proof of residence abroad (utility bill, lease agreement), and a completed NICOP application form (available at the embassy or online at nadra.gov.pk).

Application Process

You can apply through a Pakistani Embassy or Consulate in your country of residence, or for some countries, through the online Pak-Identity portal. Visit the embassy website to confirm which method is available in your location, check if appointment booking is required, and gather all necessary documents before visiting in person.

NICOP Renewal and Modification

Renewing an existing NICOP follows the same fee structure as a new application. Small modifications (name correction, address update) may have different rates — consult your local embassy for specifics. If your NICOP is lost or damaged, you'll apply for a duplicate at standard renewal rates.

Note: Exchange rates fluctuate, so local currency equivalents are approximate. Some embassies add a small service fee ($5-$20). Always confirm exact fees with your nearest Pakistani Embassy or Consulate before submitting your application. Fees and procedures are subject to change — verify at nadra.gov.pk for the latest information.
