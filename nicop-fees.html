NICOP Fees 2026

LAST CHECKED · JULY 2026

NADRA divides the world into two zones for NICOP fee purposes. Your fee depends on which zone your country of residence falls in. All fees are charged in USD unless you are applying at a Pakistani mission abroad (where local currency applies).
