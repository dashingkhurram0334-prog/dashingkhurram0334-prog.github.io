NADRA Card Fees, Made Simple

Every current charge for Smart CNIC, renewal, B-Form and FRC — normal, urgent and executive — in one place.

LAST CHECKED · 2026

Every year, thousands of Pakistanis visit their nearest NADRA Registration Center only to find that the fee they expected to pay has changed, or that they didn't bring the right combination of documents. NADRA revises its fee structure periodically, and the difference between "Normal," "Urgent," and "Executive" processing can mean waiting anywhere from a week to over a month for the same card. This guide exists to take the guesswork out of that visit — with current fee tables, document checklists, and processing timelines for every major NADRA and passport service, all verified against multiple current sources and updated regularly.

Whether you're applying for your first Smart CNIC, renewing an expired card, registering a newborn's B-Form, correcting a date of birth, or applying for an e-Passport, the fee you pay depends on a combination of factors: the type of document, how quickly you need it, whether it's a first-time application or a replacement, and in some cases, your age or location. Below, you'll find a breakdown of each of these services along with practical guidance on which processing speed makes sense for your situation.

Juvenile Card (JV Card) — Birth to 18 Years

A smart identity card for children from birth up to 18 years of age. Once the child turns 18, a fresh CNIC application must be submitted — the JV Card does not convert automatically.

Processing Fee (PKR) Timeline
Normal 750 ≈ 31 working days
Urgent 1,500 15–23 working days
Executive 2,500 7–9 working days

Required Documents

  • If child already has a B-Form: Original B-Form + valid CNIC/NICOP of one parent
  • If child has no B-Form: Computerized Birth Certificate (issued by Union Council, Municipal Committee, or Cantonment Board)
  • School Certificate (optional, helps with age verification)
  • If both parents are deceased or child is adopted: Guardianship Certificate from a competent court
  • Child's physical presence is mandatory for photo and fingerprint capture

Benefits

  • Valid photo ID for domestic travel
  • Required by many banks for opening a minor's savings account
  • Accepted by exam boards (BISE) for identity verification
  • Applying does not carry over to CNIC automatically — a fresh CNIC application is required at age 18

Understanding NADRA's Fee Structure

Why fees vary, and how to choose the right processing speed for your situation.

Why does NADRA charge different rates for the same card?
NADRA's fee structure is built around processing speed rather than the type of document itself. "Normal" processing uses standard batch printing at regional facilities, which takes longer but costs less. "Urgent" and "Executive" processing prioritizes your application in the queue and, in many centers, prints the card at a dedicated facility — which is why it costs more but is delivered faster. This system exists so that people who need a card immediately (for travel, a job interview, or an emergency) have that option, while those who can wait aren't forced to pay a premium.

How do I decide which processing speed to choose?
If you have no immediate deadline — for example, you're renewing a card that's still valid for another month — Normal processing is the most economical choice. If you have a specific date you need the card by (a flight, a bank appointment, a job requirement), count backward from that date and add a few extra days as a buffer, since processing times are estimates rather than guarantees. Executive processing is generally reserved for genuine emergencies, as it costs significantly more than Normal.

What happens if my application is rejected or delayed?
NADRA occasionally holds applications for manual verification — this is more common for age corrections, name changes, or cases where family tree records don't match. If your application is delayed beyond the stated timeline, you can track its status online or visit your registration center with your token number. Fees already paid are generally not refunded even if additional documents are requested, so it helps to bring more documentation than you think you'll need on your first visit.

Are these fees the same all over Pakistan?
Yes — NADRA fees are set nationally and don't vary by province or city. However, processing times can vary slightly depending on how busy your local registration center is; major urban centers sometimes have longer queues even for the same processing tier.
