Children's Passport Fee

Children without a CNIC can apply using the 13-digit registration number on their B-Form, CRC, or Smart CNIC/NICOP. Children under 15 are only eligible for the 5-year validity passport.

Lost Passport Replacement

Note: Fees are fixed nationwide and do not include courier or home delivery charges. DGIP fees are non-refundable once paid. Always confirm current rates on the official DGIP portal (dgip.gov.pk) before paying.
