Complete Pakistani Passport Guide 2026

FEES · DOCUMENTS · CHILD FORMS · PROCESS 2026

Applying for a Pakistani passport is straightforward when you have the right information and documents. Whether you need a new passport, want to renew an expired one, or require urgent issuance, this comprehensive guide covers everything from accurate fee structures for all passport types to required documents and special procedures for CNIC modifications before passport application. We also cover child applications using B-Forms and JV cards, working day timelines, and the complete step-by-step application process.

Pakistani Passport Fee Structure 2026

Pakistani passport fees vary based on three factors: the number of pages (36, 72, or 100), the validity period you choose (5 or 10 years), and your preferred processing speed (Normal or Urgent). E-Passports with biometric security are available at higher fees. Here are the complete and accurate fees for 2026.

For a 36-page Standard Passport with 5 years validity, you will pay Rs. 4,500 for Normal processing (10-15 working days) or Rs. 7,500 for Urgent processing (5-7 working days). If you prefer 10 years validity on a 36-page passport, the Normal fee is Rs. 6,700 and Urgent is Rs. 11,200.

For a 72-page Standard Passport with 5 years validity, Normal processing costs Rs. 8,200 and Urgent costs Rs. 13,500. The 10-year validity option on a 72-page passport is Rs. 12,400 for Normal and Rs. 20,200 for Urgent processing.

For a 100-page Standard Passport with 5 years validity, you will pay Rs. 9,000 for Normal or Rs. 18,000 for Urgent. The 10-year validity version costs Rs. 13,500 (Normal) or Rs. 27,000 (Urgent). Note that 100-page passports are typically for frequent international travelers who accumulate many visa stamps.

E-Passport (Electronic/Biometric) options are also available and recommended for security reasons. A 36-page E-Passport with 5 years validity costs Rs. 9,000 (Normal) or Rs. 15,000 (Urgent), while the 10-year version is Rs. 13,500 (Normal) or Rs. 22,500 (Urgent). For a 72-page E-Passport with 5 years validity, expect Rs. 16,500 (Normal) or Rs. 27,000 (Urgent). The 10-year 72-page E-Passport is the most expensive option at Rs. 24,750 (Normal) or Rs. 40,500 (Urgent). E-Passports feature embedded biometric data (fingerprints and iris scan) stored in a security microchip and are increasingly preferred by international immigration authorities.

Processing Timeline and Working Days

Processing times are measured in working days (Monday through Friday, excluding national holidays and official office closures). Normal processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days, which translates to approximately 2-3 weeks in calendar days depending on weekends and holidays. Urgent processing is faster at 5 to 7 working days, equivalent to roughly 1-2 weeks. It's important to note that during peak travel seasons (summer school holidays, Eid holidays, and year-end), processing times may extend by an additional 2-3 days due to high volume. National holidays and unexpected office closures will also affect the timeline, so applying in advance of your travel date is always wise.

Required Documents for Passport Application

Gathering the correct documents before visiting the passport office is essential to avoid delays or rejection. You will need your original computerized CNIC (National ID Card) along with photocopies of both the front and back. Your CNIC must be currently valid; expired CNICs will require renewal first through NADRA before you can proceed with passport application. Bring an original birth certificate or a certified copy from your local Union Council. You will need four recent, colored passport-sized photographs measuring 4x6 centimeters with a white background, all taken within the last three months. Photographs must show your face clearly with neutral expression, proper lighting, and without glasses or head covering (except for religious reasons, though this may require additional verification).

Complete the appropriate application form: Form A for new passport applicants or Form B for renewals. These forms are available at all passport offices or downloadable from www.passport.gov.pk. You must obtain a Character Certificate from either a gazetted officer, notary public, or senior government official such as a school principal, college principal, or bank manager attesting to your good character.

If your CNIC photograph shows significant facial changes or poor image quality compared to your current appearance, you will need to prepare an affidavit explaining the facial changes (due to aging, weight gain or loss, hairstyle change, etc.). Have this affidavit notarized by a notary public or gazetted officer. If you are married or have changed your name due to marriage, bring your original marriage certificate or certified copy from the Union Council. While increasingly not strictly enforced, it's safer to bring a Domicile Certificate issued by your local union council or district administration as proof of provincial residence.

CNIC Facial Issues and Modification Before Passport

Pakistani immigration authorities conduct facial matching between your passport photograph and your CNIC photograph. If your CNIC shows an outdated or significantly different facial image, passport officials may question the discrepancy or delay your application. To avoid this, modify your CNIC photograph before applying for a passport. The process is straightforward: visit your nearest NADRA registration center with your current CNIC, a fresh photograph taken within three months with proper lighting and white background, a notarized affidavit explaining your facial changes, and a character certificate. NADRA staff will update your photograph, capture new biometric data (fingerprints and iris scan), and reissue your CNIC within 3-5 working days. Once you collect your updated CNIC with the new photograph, you can proceed with confidence to the passport office knowing your documents are consistent.

Child Passport Applications: B-Form and JV Card Holders

Children under 18 years holding a B-Form (Child Registration Certificate) can apply for a passport with proper documentation and parental consent. You will need the original B-Form, photocopies of both parents' CNICs, the child's birth certificate, four recent photographs of the child, a character certificate from the child's school principal, and a notarized letter of parental consent. Both parents should ideally accompany the child to the passport office for the biometric collection and interview, though in some cases one parent's signature on the consent letter may suffice. Processing follows the standard timeline: Normal processing takes 10-15 working days and Urgent processing takes 5-7 working days.

JV Card (Jildaat-e-Virat/Temporary Card) holders who are 18 or older can also apply for passports if they meet other eligibility criteria. Required documents include the original JV card, a detailed affidavit explaining your JV status and the reason why you do not hold a regular CNIC, your birth certificate, four photographs, a character certificate, and your domicile certificate. The application process mirrors that of regular passport applicants, though officers may conduct additional verification given your temporary ID status.

Once a child with a B-Form reaches age 18, they become eligible for a regular Smart CNIC. At that point, they should apply for CNIC issuance if they haven't already, and then use the CNIC (not the B-Form) for future passport applications or renewals.

Step-by-Step Passport Application Process

1

Gather and Organize Documents

Collect your CNIC (original and photocopies), birth certificate, character certificate, four photographs, completed application form, affidavit (if needed), and marriage certificate (if applicable). Arrange them in the order specified by your local passport office.

2

Visit Your Regional Passport Office

Locate and visit your nearest passport office during operating hours (typically 9 AM to 4 PM, Monday through Friday). Most major cities have dedicated passport offices. Bring all documents organized and ready.

3

Submit Application at Intake Counter

Present your application form and documents to the intake counter. Staff will review for completeness, verify your identity, and assign you an application reference number. Keep this reference number and your receipt safely for tracking purposes.

4

Pay the Applicable Fee

Proceed to the payment counter and pay the fee corresponding to your chosen page count, validity period, and processing speed. Keep your receipt as it contains your reference number and proof of payment.

5

Attend Biometric and Interview Appointment

Return to the office at your scheduled date for biometric data collection (fingerprints and iris scan) and a brief interview. Officers will verify your identity and ask about your passport purpose and travel plans. This process typically takes 10-15 minutes.

6

Collect Your Passport

Once your passport is ready (as per the processing timeline you selected), return to the office with your receipt. Verify all details are correct, including your name spelling, CNIC number, passport number, and validity dates, before leaving the office.

Online Passport Application Portal

Pakistan now offers an online application system at www.passport.gov.pk where you can apply without an initial in-person visit. Register with your email and phone number, complete your profile with personal information, upload scanned copies of your documents (CNIC, birth certificate, photographs), select your desired passport office and processing speed, and pay the fee online via bank transfer or credit card. You will then receive an appointment slot for biometric collection and interview. After attending your appointment and completing the interview process, you can collect your passport. The online system significantly reduces paperwork and waiting times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I apply for a passport if my CNIC is expired?
A: No. Your CNIC must be valid and current at the time of passport application. Renew your CNIC first through NADRA, then proceed with your passport application.
Q: Should I get my CNIC photograph updated before applying for passport?
A: If your CNIC photograph is outdated or shows significant facial differences, yes. Officers compare CNIC and passport photos. Updating your CNIC first prevents delays or questions.
Q: How long is a Pakistani passport valid?
A: You can choose either 5 or 10 years validity when applying. Both are standard options with different fee levels. After expiry, you must apply for renewal.
Q: Can I track my passport application online?
A: Yes. Use your application reference number on www.passport.gov.pk to check status, or visit your passport office in person.
Q: I need a passport within one week for urgent travel. What should I do?
A: Apply for Urgent processing (5-7 working days). If you have an emergency, contact your local passport office directly; some offices provide expedited processing in genuine emergencies.
Q: Can children under 18 apply for passports with B-Forms?
A: Yes. Children can apply with a B-Form, parental consent documentation, and guardian CNIC. Parents or guardians must accompany them for biometric and interview.
