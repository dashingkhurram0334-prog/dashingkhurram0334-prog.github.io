NADRA Card Fees 2026 — Complete Guide

Understand how to check NADRA card fees, processing categories and service charges without relying on outdated information.

1. Why NADRA fees can change

Government service fees are not necessarily permanent. Authorities can revise charges, introduce service categories or change delivery arrangements. This means an amount copied from an older article may no longer be accurate.

2. What affects the amount

  • The type of identity service
  • Normal or faster processing category, where offered
  • Delivery or collection option
  • Any clearly stated additional service charge

3. How to verify the current fee

Start with the official NADRA information for the exact service you need. Compare the service name and processing category, not just the number. If an unofficial website and the official source disagree, the official source should be treated as the final authority.

4. Before making payment

  1. Confirm the service you selected.
  2. Check the current fee.
  3. Confirm the processing option.
  4. Keep the payment/receipt reference.
  5. Do not send payment to an unknown person offering unofficial processing.

5. Why old fee tables are risky

Fee tables are often copied from one website to another without a date or source. Readers may therefore see the same outdated figure repeated many times. A large number of websites showing the same amount does not make that amount official.

6. Practical checklist

Before applying, write down the exact service you need, verify the current official fee, check the document requirements, and keep your receipt. This simple checklist can prevent avoidable confusion.

7. Frequently asked questions

Is one fee applicable to every CNIC service?

No. Different services and processing categories may have different charges.

Can FeeGuide guarantee a fee?

No. We explain how to verify current information; the official authority controls its fees.
