CNIC Application Guide — First-Time Applicants

A complete walkthrough of applying for your first Smart CNIC, from eligibility to card delivery.

Last checked · 2026·Independent Guide

Who Needs to Apply for a CNIC?

Every Pakistani citizen who reaches the age of 18 is required to obtain a CNIC under the NADRA Ordinance 2000. The Smart CNIC is the primary identity document used for banking, property transactions, SIM registration, employment verification, government services, and international travel documentation.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Applicant must be a Pakistani citizen by birth or naturalization
  • Applicant must be 18 years of age or older
  • Applicant must have a valid B-Form or Juvenile Card if transitioning from the child registry
  • Applicant's family tree must already be registered in the NADRA database through a parent or guardian's CNIC

Step-by-Step Application Process

  1. Register online — Create an account on the Pak-Identity portal and select "New CNIC Application."
  2. Fill the form — Enter personal details exactly as they appear on your B-Form and family CNIC records.
  3. Upload documents — Scan and upload your B-Form, parents' CNIC copies, and a recent photograph.
  4. Book a token — Select your nearest NADRA Registration Center (NRC) and choose an appointment slot.
  5. Visit for biometrics — Attend in person for fingerprint scanning, iris capture, and photograph verification.
  6. Pay the fee — Settle payment based on your chosen processing tier.
  7. Track and collect — Use your token number to monitor status and collect the card once ready.

Documents Required

  • Original B-Form or Juvenile Registration Certificate
  • Original CNIC of father or mother
  • Original CNIC of a witness (if required for verification)
  • Recent passport-sized photograph with white background

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many first-time applicants face delays because of mismatched spellings between their B-Form and their parents' CNIC records, blurry document scans, or missing witness signatures. Double-check every field against your official documents before submitting, since corrections after submission can add weeks to the process.

If your family tree has not been updated in NADRA's database — for example, after a parent's CNIC renewal — resolve that first, as it is one of the most common reasons for application holds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for CNIC without a witness?

In most cases a witness is not mandatory for standard applications if the family tree is already properly linked, but NRC staff may request one in specific cases.

What if I lose my token slip before biometrics?

You can retrieve your token number through your Pak-Identity account or by contacting the NADRA helpline.
