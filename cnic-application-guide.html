Complete CNIC Application Guide 2026

STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS · UPDATED JULY 2026

Applying for your first Smart CNIC (National Identity Card) in Pakistan might seem overwhelming, but with the right information and documents, the process becomes straightforward. This comprehensive guide walks you through every step — from eligibility requirements to receiving your card in hand.

Who is Eligible for CNIC?

You are eligible to apply for a Smart CNIC if you meet these criteria:

✓

Age: You must be at least 18 years old at the time of application. Those under 18 receive a B-Form (Child Registration Certificate) instead.

✓

Citizenship: You must be a Pakistani citizen by birth or naturalization.

✓

Residency: You must be a resident of Pakistan or an overseas Pakistani applying through a Pakistani mission abroad (embassy or consulate).

✓

No prior CNIC: You should not already hold a valid Smart CNIC. If your card has expired or been lost, you'll apply for renewal or a duplicate instead.

Documents You'll Need

Before heading to a NADRA center, gather all required documents. Missing even one can delay your application.

✓

Proof of Birth: Either a Birth Certificate (from the Union Council) or a B-Form issued by NADRA. If you don't have one, you'll need to first obtain it.

✓

Proof of Citizenship: Your passport (if you have one), or an older national ID, or a citizenship certificate.

✓

Parent's or Guardian's Documentation: If you're applying for the first time, NADRA will verify family lineage. Bring your parents' or guardian's CNIC copies.

✓

Address Proof: A utility bill (electricity, gas, or water) dated within the last three months showing your current address, OR a recent mobile phone bill.

✓

Photographs: Two passport-sized colored photographs (4x6 cm) with a white background, taken within the last three months.

Step-by-Step Application Process

Step 1 — Visit Your Nearest NADRA Center

Locate the NADRA Registration Center closest to you. You can find a list of all centers at nadra.gov.pk or call their helpline at 051-111-786-100. Most major cities have multiple centers; visit during office hours (typically 9 AM to 4 PM, Monday to Friday).

Step 2 — Collect Your Registration Token

Upon arrival, go to the token counter and inform staff you're applying for a new Smart CNIC. They'll assign you a token number based on processing speed: Normal, Urgent, or Executive. Each token comes with a fee. Write down your token number — you'll need it throughout the process.

Step 3 — Document Verification

Wait for your token number to be called at the document verification window. A NADRA staff member will review all your documents to ensure they're complete and authentic. If anything is missing or unclear, they'll let you know immediately. Double-check everything is present before you leave this stage.

Step 4 — Biometric Data Collection

Once documents are verified, you'll proceed to the biometric window where they capture your fingerprints (all 10 fingers), photograph, and iris scan. Keep your hands clean and dry for proper fingerprint capture. This process takes about 10-15 minutes.

Step 5 — Payment

Proceed to the cash counter to pay the applicable fee based on your chosen processing speed. Normal processing is Rs. 750, Urgent is Rs. 1,500, and Executive is Rs. 2,500. Keep your receipt — it contains your reference number for tracking.

Step 6 — Receive Your Slip and Go Home

After payment, you'll receive a slip with your application reference number and expected delivery date. This is critical — save it safely. Your CNIC will be ready by the date mentioned or earlier if you chose Urgent or Executive processing.

How to Track Your CNIC

Once you've applied, you don't have to wait passively. You can track your application status using three methods:

1

SMS Tracking

Send your 13-digit tracking ID (from your slip) via SMS to 8009. NADRA will reply with your current status: "Data Entry," "Processing," "Printing," or "Ready for Delivery."

2

Online Portal

Visit id.nadra.gov.pk, enter your tracking ID, and check your status in real-time. The portal is available 24/7.

3

NADRA Mobile App (Pak-ID)

Download the free Pak-ID app from Google Play or Apple App Store, sign in, and track all your identity documents from your phone.

Collecting Your CNIC

Once your card is ready (NADRA will notify you), return to the same NADRA center with your receipt/reference number. The delivery counter will verify your identity using your biometric data and hand over your card. This final step takes just a few minutes. Always check that your card details are correct before leaving — if there's any error, report it immediately so it can be corrected.

Common Issues & How to Avoid Them

Rejected Application: Applications are rarely rejected outright, but they can be delayed if documents don't match or if lineage cannot be verified. Ensure all documents are current and authentic.

Delayed Processing: Sometimes processing takes longer than quoted if there are verification delays. Check your status regularly using the tracking methods above. If significantly delayed (beyond 40-50 days for Normal processing), visit the center in person to inquire.

Lost Receipt: If you lose your reference slip, NADRA staff can look up your application using your name, father's name, and date of application. Bring any proof of application (like SMS confirmation if you used it).

Wrong Details on Card: If your CNIC contains errors, visit a NADRA center immediately with the card and your original documents. Corrections are usually made within 7 days at no extra cost.

Important: This guide covers the standard CNIC application process as of July 2026. Procedures may occasionally change, and requirements can vary slightly by province. Always verify current requirements at your local NADRA center or at nadra.gov.pk before applying. For official queries or if you face any complications, contact NADRA's helpline at 051-111-786-100.
