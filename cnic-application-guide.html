CNIC Application Guide 2026

This independent guide explains the usual application process, documents, current Smart CNIC fees and ways to verify status. Requirements can vary by case, so confirm before visiting a centre.

Important: The fee table below follows NADRA's current published fee structure. The official NADRA fee page lists New Smart NIC at PKR 750 / 1,500 / 2,500 for Normal / Urgent / Executive, with published priority timelines of 31 / 23 / 09 days. Always re-check the official fee page before payment.

Who can apply?

A first-time adult Pakistani citizen generally applies for a CNIC through the NADRA registration process. For online PakID cases, first-time CNIC applications may require a blood relative's account and an in-person visit to complete the process.

Documents to prepare

  • Birth/citizenship evidence as applicable to your case.
  • Parent or blood-relative CNIC information when required for first-time registration.
  • Any additional supporting documents requested by NADRA for your specific case.

NADRA's published priority timelines for these Smart NIC services are 31 days (Normal), 23 days (Urgent), and 09 days (Executive). Timelines can change.

Application process

  1. Check the requirements for your specific CNIC service.
  2. Visit a NADRA Registration Centre or use the official PakID route when eligible.
  3. Complete identity/document verification and biometric capture as instructed.
  4. Pay the displayed applicable fee and keep your receipt/reference details.
  5. Use the official tracking method to follow the application.

How to verify your application

For current tracking instructions, use NADRA's official support and PakID resources. NADRA's published Services Charter lists 8400 as the CNIC application tracking SMS short code.
