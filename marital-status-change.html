Marital Status Change on CNIC 2026 — Documents Required

Last checked · 2026  ·  Independent Guide

Getting married or finalizing a divorce is one of the most common reasons Pakistanis need to update their Smart CNIC. NADRA records marital status directly on the card and in its central database, so the change isn't just a formality — it affects everything from passport applications to family registration certificates for future children. This guide walks through exactly what documents you need, how the process works, and what to expect at the registration center.

Why Marital Status Needs to Be Updated

Your CNIC's marital status field feeds into several other NADRA-linked processes. If it isn't updated after marriage, you may run into complications when applying for a spouse's Family Registration Certificate (FRC), adding a spouse's name to your family tree in NADRA's records, or applying for a child's B-Form later on. Divorce updates matter too, particularly for anyone who needs to remove a former spouse's name from official family records or update their status before remarrying.

Documents Required After Marriage

The exact paperwork depends on whether you're updating your own CNIC after marriage or adding a spouse who is a foreign national. For a standard case where both parties are Pakistani citizens, NADRA typically asks for:

  • Original Nikah Nama (marriage certificate), registered with the relevant Union Council
  • Original CNIC of the applicant (to be updated)
  • Original CNIC of the spouse
  • Passport-sized photograph meeting NADRA specifications
  • Union Council registration number of the Nikah Nama, if requested for verification

If your spouse is a foreign national, you'll also need their passport, proof of their nationality, and in some cases an attested marriage certificate recognized by both countries. Because these cases are assessed individually, it's worth confirming the exact list with your local NADRA office before your appointment.

Documents Required After Divorce

Updating your status after a divorce follows a similar logic but requires legal proof that the marriage has been formally dissolved. NADRA generally asks for:

  • Original divorce certificate (Talaq Nama) issued and registered by the Union Council
  • Original CNIC of the applicant
  • Court order or decree, if the divorce was finalized through the family court rather than an out-of-court Talaq process
  • Passport-sized photograph as per NADRA specifications

Where children are involved, NADRA may also ask for supporting documents relating to custody arrangements, particularly if those children's B-Forms need to be updated separately afterward.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Log in to your account on the Pak-Identity portal, or create one if you don't already have one
  2. Select "Family Registration Certificate" or "Marital Status Update" under CNIC modification services
  3. Upload scanned copies of your Nikah Nama or divorce certificate along with your existing CNIC details
  4. Choose your processing speed — Normal, Urgent, or Executive
  5. Pay the applicable fee through the portal
  6. Visit your nearest NADRA registration center for biometric verification and document submission of originals
  7. Collect your updated Smart CNIC once processing is complete, either in person or via courier depending on the option selected

Processing Fee

The fee for a marital status update depends on your gender, because of how NADRA's system treats the marital status field differently for men and women.

For Male Applicants

On a male CNIC holder's record, marital status is treated as a non-printable field — it's stored in NADRA's database but doesn't require the physical card to be reprinted. Because of this, the update is a simple database correction rather than a full modification.

For Female Applicants

On a female CNIC holder's record, marital status is a printable field, so the update requires the card itself to be reprinted with the new details. This is charged at the standard CNIC modification rates, based on processing speed:

Because a new card is printed for female applicants, the standard CNIC modification fee applies. For male applicants, since the card isn't reprinted, only the flat Rs. 100 database update charge applies. Always confirm the current fee on the Pak-Identity portal before paying, as NADRA periodically revises its fee schedule.

Common Issues and How to Avoid Them

Union Council record mismatch

The single biggest cause of delay is a mismatch between the name or CNIC number on the Nikah Nama or divorce certificate and NADRA's existing record. Double-check spellings and CNIC numbers on your marriage or divorce paperwork before submitting, and get corrections made at the Union Council first if needed.

Unregistered Nikah Nama

A Nikah Nama that hasn't been formally registered with the Union Council generally won't be accepted. If your marriage was conducted informally, register it with the Union Council before starting the NADRA update process.

Missing spouse CNIC

If your spouse's CNIC isn't available at the time of application — for example, if they're abroad — the update can be delayed. Where possible, gather both CNICs before booking your appointment to avoid a second visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to update my CNIC after marriage?

It isn't legally mandatory to update it immediately, but doing so is strongly recommended since many downstream services — spouse's FRC, children's B-Forms, joint bank accounts — rely on NADRA's records reflecting your current marital status accurately.

Can both spouses update their CNICs in one visit?

Yes. If both spouses are present with their required documents, most NADRA centers can process both updates in the same visit, though each is treated as a separate application with its own fee.

How long does the update take to reflect after divorce?

Once your application is verified and biometrics are captured, the marital status change typically reflects in NADRA's system within the same timeframe as your chosen processing tier — Normal, Urgent, or Executive.
