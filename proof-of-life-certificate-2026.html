NADRA Proof of Life Certificate 2026 — Apply Free Online for Pensioners

NADRA has introduced the Proof of Life Certificate for pensioners, available free of cost through Pak-Identity — apply from home without visiting an NRC office.

Updated for 2026·3 min read

What Is the Proof of Life Certificate?

The Proof of Life Certificate is a new NADRA-issued document that pensioners use to confirm they are alive, which is often required by pension-disbursing departments to continue releasing pension payments. It is issued free of cost.

How to Apply Online (No Own Account Needed)

The certificate can be generated through Pak-Identity. If the pensioner does not have their own online account, a blood relative — such as a son, daughter, sister, or wife — can generate it using their own account by entering the pensioner's CNIC number.

  • Log in to Pak-Identity: A blood relative (son, daughter, sister, wife, etc.) logs into their own Pak-Identity account.
  • Enter the pensioner's CNIC number: The relative enters the pensioner's CNIC number in the relevant section to request the certificate.
  • Certificate generated free of cost: The Proof of Life Certificate is issued at no charge, directly from home.

Alternative: Apply In Person

  • Nearest NRC Office: Pensioners can visit any NADRA Registration Center to get the certificate issued in person.
  • NADRA e-Sahulat Franchise: The certificate can also be obtained through any authorized NADRA e-Sahulat franchise outlet.
Note: This is free of cost through all three methods — online via Pak-Identity, at an NRC office, or at an e-Sahulat franchise. No fee should be charged for this certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own Pak-Identity account to get this certificate?

No. If the pensioner doesn't have their own account, a blood relative (son, daughter, sister, wife, etc.) can generate the certificate using their own Pak-Identity account by entering the pensioner's CNIC number.

Is there any fee for the Proof of Life Certificate?

No, it is issued free of cost — whether applied for online, at an NRC office, or through an e-Sahulat franchise.

Who typically needs this certificate?

Pensioners are typically required to submit this certificate to their pension-disbursing department to confirm they are alive and continue receiving pension payments.
