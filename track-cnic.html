How to Track Your CNIC Application

GUIDE · 2026

Once you've submitted your CNIC or Smart CNIC application, you can track its processing status through any of the methods below.

Method 1 — SMS Tracking

1

Send your tracking ID via SMS

Send your 13-digit tracking number (printed on your application receipt) to 8009.

SMS: [Tracking ID] → 8009
2

Receive status reply

NADRA replies with your current status — e.g. "Data Entry," "Processing," "Printing," or "Ready for Delivery."

Method 2 — Pak-Identity Portal

1

Visit id.nadra.gov.pk

Log in with your registered account credentials.

2

Open "Track Application"

Enter your tracking ID to see a detailed status timeline.

Method 3 — NADRA App

1

Download the official NADRA Pak-Identity app

Available on Google Play and the App Store.

2

Enter your tracking ID under "Track Status"

The app shows the same real-time status as the SMS and web portal.

Note: Processing times shown while tracking are estimates and can shift due to verification backlogs. If your status hasn't changed in over 30 days, visiting your nearest NADRA Registration Center in person is the fastest way to get an update.
