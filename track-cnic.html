How to Track Your CNIC Application — 3 Methods

TRACKING GUIDE · JULY 2026

Once you've submitted your Smart CNIC application at a NADRA center, you don't have to wait passively wondering when it will be ready. NADRA provides three convenient ways to track your application in real-time — via SMS, an online portal, or a mobile app — so you always know exactly where your card is in the processing pipeline.

Method 1 — SMS Tracking (Instant, No Internet Needed)

Send SMS to 8009

The simplest tracking method. When you submit your CNIC application, you'll receive a reference slip with a 13-digit tracking ID. Keep this safe.

SMS: [Your 13-digit ID] → 8009

Example: If your tracking ID is 1234567890123, text "1234567890123" to 8009. Within seconds, NADRA will reply with your current status.

Pros: Works without internet, instant response, free (standard SMS rates apply), available 24/7. Cons: Limited detail — only shows current status, not historical updates.

Method 2 — Online Portal (Real-Time, Detailed)

Visit id.nadra.gov.pk

Go to the official NADRA portal, select "Track Your Card," enter your 13-digit tracking ID, and you'll see detailed information about your application.

What you'll see: Current processing stage, estimated completion date, and historical status updates for each step.

Pros: Most detailed information, 24/7 access, free, accessible from any device with internet. Cons: Requires internet, sometimes shows information with slight delay (usually updates within 24 hours).

Method 3 — Mobile App (Comprehensive, User-Friendly)

Download Pak-ID App

Download the free "Pak-ID" app from Google Play Store (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS). Create an account using your email and phone number, then log in.

Inside the app: View all your identity documents (CNIC, passport status, B-Form), track multiple applications at once, receive push notifications when status changes, and manage renewal reminders.

Pros: User-friendly interface, real-time notifications, track multiple documents, no tracking ID memorization needed. Cons: Requires smartphone and app installation, uses mobile data.

Understanding Your CNIC Status

As your application moves through NADRA's system, you'll see status updates. Here's what each stage means:

Data Entry
Your application is being entered into the system. This stage typically takes 1-3 days.
Processing
NADRA is verifying your documents and checking records. This is the longest stage, lasting 10-20 days depending on processing type.
Printing
Your card has been approved and is being physically printed. Takes 2-5 days.
Ready for Delivery
Your card is complete and waiting for you to collect. You can now visit the center and pick it up using your receipt.

Troubleshooting Tracking Issues

Q: My tracking ID doesn't work. What do I do?
A: Ensure you're entering exactly 13 digits without spaces or dashes. Double-check your reference slip for typos. If the ID is still invalid, visit a NADRA center with your receipt and staff can look up your application manually using your name and date of application.
Q: The portal says "Data Entry" but 3 days have passed. Why is it slow?
A: Data entry typically completes within 1-3 days, but delays occasionally happen due to high volume. If it's been more than 5 days, visit the center to confirm your application was properly submitted. The submission slip should have been stamped and dated.
Q: My status hasn't changed in a week. Is something wrong?
A: Status updates can lag by 24-48 hours due to system synchronization. Wait another day and check again. If it still hasn't progressed after 10 days (or the quoted timeline for your processing type), visit the center to inquire. Sometimes documents need additional verification, which causes delays.
Q: What if my application shows an error or "Rejected" status?
A: Visit the center immediately with your receipt. A NADRA officer will explain the rejection reason. Usually, rejections are fixable — you'll reapply with corrected documents. See our "CNIC Rejected? Why & Solutions" guide for detailed troubleshooting.
Q: How often should I check my status?
A: Checking every 2-3 days is reasonable. Checking multiple times daily is unnecessary since updates typically happen overnight. If you have the Pak-ID app installed, you'll receive automatic notifications when status changes, so you don't need to manually check.
Pro tip: Save your 13-digit tracking ID in a safe place or take a photo of it. You'll need it multiple times — to track progress, at collection time, and potentially for customer service inquiries. Keep your original reference slip safe as well.
