Documents Required

GUIDE · 2026

Here's what you'll typically need to bring to your nearest NADRA Registration Center, by application type.

Smart CNIC — New Application (First Time)

✓

Original B-Form or Child Registration Certificate

✓

Father's original CNIC (and mother's, if available)

✓

Proof of residence (utility bill or rent agreement)

✓

Educational certificate, if applicable

Smart CNIC — Renewal

✓

Original expired/expiring CNIC

✓

Updated proof of residence if your address has changed

Duplicate CNIC (Lost or Damaged)

✓

Damaged card (if applicable) or an affidavit/report for a lost card

✓

Any other government-issued ID as secondary proof, if available

B-Form (Child Registration Certificate)

✓

Original hospital birth certificate or Union Council birth registration

✓

Both parents' original CNICs

Family Registration Certificate (FRC)

✓

Applicant's original CNIC

✓

CNICs of family members being listed on the certificate

Note: Document requirements can vary slightly by case (e.g. late birth registration, name corrections). Confirm the exact list for your situation with your nearest NADRA Registration Center before visiting.
