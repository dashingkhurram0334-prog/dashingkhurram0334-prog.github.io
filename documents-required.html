CNIC Documents Required 2026 — Complete Checklist

DETAILED GUIDE · JULY 2026

Applying for a Smart CNIC in Pakistan requires gathering specific documents. Missing even one can delay your application or cause rejection. This comprehensive guide explains each document type in detail, what NADRA accepts, and how to prepare them before visiting a NADRA center.

Primary Identity Documents

1. Birth Certificate (Required)

NADRA's primary proof of your identity and date of birth. You need an original or certified copy from the Union Council where you were born (not just a photocopy). If your birth wasn't registered at birth, you can apply for late registration through the Union Council, which may take 2-3 weeks.

✓ Original or certified copy from Union Council
✓ Must show your name, father's name, date of birth, and place of birth
✓ If registered late (after 1 year of birth), bring proof of late registration application

2. Previous CNIC or Passport (if applicable)

If you've held a CNIC or passport before, bring the original copy. This speeds up verification since NADRA already has your biometric data on file. If expired, it's still acceptable for reference purposes.

✓ Original card/passport even if expired
✓ Both copies if you have multiple types of ID

Family Documentation

3. Parents' CNIC Copies (Required)

NADRA verifies your lineage by checking your parents' records. Bring photocopies of both parents' valid CNICs. If one parent is deceased, bring their original CNIC or a certified copy. If a parent never obtained a CNIC, visit NADRA with their birth certificate and any available identification.

✓ Clear photocopies of both parents' CNIC front and back
✓ Deceased parent's CNIC or certified copy from family records office
✓ If parents have different surnames, bring marriage certificate or relevant documentation explaining the name change

4. Family Registration Certificate (FRC) — Recommended

An FRC issued by NADRA showing your complete family lineage (parents, siblings, spouse if married) strengthens your application significantly by confirming family relationships. You can obtain an FRC at any NADRA center for Rs. 1,000 (standard) or Rs. 2,000 (combined alpha-beta).

✓ NADRA-issued FRC (not a house-hold register from Union Council)
✓ Shows your lineage clearly with NADRA seal

Proof of Address

5. Address Proof (Required)

NADRA requires recent proof that you currently live at the address you're declaring. Utility bills are most accepted. The document must be dated within the last 3 months and clearly show your name and full address.

✓ Electricity bill (WAPDA, KE, or relevant provider)
✓ Gas bill (SSGC or provider)
✓ Water bill (WASA or provider)
✓ Mobile phone bill (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, etc. — must show address)
✓ Issued within the last 3 months

Alternative if no utility bill: property deed, lease agreement, or a landlord's notarized letter confirming your residency (dated within 3 months).

Photographs

6. Passport Photographs (Required)

NADRA uses these for your CNIC card printing. Photographs must meet strict requirements: colored, clear, taken recently (within 3 months), with white background, neutral expression, and proper lighting. Avoid wearing glasses, hats, or head coverings (unless for religious reasons, though this can complicate verification).

✓ Two 4x6 cm colored photographs
✓ Taken within the last 3 months
✓ White background
✓ Face should be at least 70% of photo area
✓ No glasses, no head covering (except religious)

Additional Documentation (If Applicable)

7. Marriage Certificate (If Married)

If you're married, bring an original or certified copy of your marriage certificate. NADRA needs this to verify your legal marital status and ensure no conflicting records exist for you or your spouse.

8. Name-Change Documentation (If Applicable)

If you've changed your name (due to marriage, court order, or other reasons), bring the official documentation. This includes marriage certificate (for name change after marriage) or court order (for legal name changes). Without this, NADRA will see your legal name and given name as conflicting.

9. Educational Certificates (Helpful but Not Always Required)

While not mandatory, school certificates, college degrees, or diplomas help NADRA verify your identity if other documentation is unclear. They show a consistent record of your name over time. If your birth certificate has a discrepancy with other documents, educational records can clarify.

Common Document Issues & Solutions

Issue: Birth certificate name doesn't match other documents. Solution: Bring educational records or FRC to establish the correct spelling. Visit NADRA with all documents to clarify.

Issue: Address proof is older than 3 months. Solution: Get a recent utility bill immediately (most are issued monthly). Alternatively, get a notarized letter from your landlord.

Issue: One or both parents don't have CNICs. Solution: Bring their birth certificates instead. Your parents may need to apply for CNICs too, which can be done in parallel.

Issue: Lost birth certificate. Solution: Apply for a duplicate from your Union Council (Rs. 50-100, takes 1-2 weeks). In the meantime, bring an FRC or school certificate as proof of identity.

Pro tip: Arrive at NADRA 15 minutes early with all documents arranged in order. A well-organized application package speeds up verification. Take photos of all your documents before visiting, in case NADRA keeps originals for verification (though they usually return them same-day).
