NADRA Jobs 2026 — How to Apply Online

Last checked · 2026  ·  Independent Guide

NADRA regularly hires for roles ranging from entry-level data entry positions to senior technical and management posts across its offices in Pakistan. Since every genuine opening is advertised through a single official portal, knowing exactly where to look and how to apply correctly matters more than it might seem. This guide covers the kinds of roles NADRA typically recruits for, what you'll need, and the step-by-step application process.

Official portal only: NADRA jobs are advertised and applied for exclusively at careers.nadra.gov.pk. There is no fee to apply or sit for a test, and NADRA does not recruit through WhatsApp, email, or paid agents.

Types of Positions NADRA Typically Hires For

NADRA's vacancies generally fall into a few broad categories, each with different qualification requirements:

  • Support/entry-level roles — Naib Qasid, Office Attendant, Driver, Scanning Operator
  • Data and clerical roles — Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator), Junior Clerk
  • Technical roles — Network Engineer, IT Specialist, Database Security, VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing)
  • Management roles — Assistant Director, Deputy Director, Deputy Assistant Director
  • Senior/HQ roles — Director General, Chief Technology Officer, and other leadership positions at Islamabad HQ

NADRA Technologies Limited (NTL), NADRA's technology subsidiary, also recruits separately for specialized technical roles — these are listed on the same careers portal.

Step-by-Step: How to Apply for a NADRA Job

  1. Visit the official careers portal at careers.nadra.gov.pk.
  2. Browse current vacancies, or click "View Vacancies" to see the full list of open positions.
  3. Create an account using a valid email address and mobile number, if you haven't already.
  4. Select the position you're eligible for and review its specific requirements.
  5. Fill out the online application form carefully with accurate personal, educational, and contact details.
  6. Upload the required documents (see checklist below) as clear scanned copies or photos.
  7. Review your application thoroughly before the final submission — most NADRA application forms cannot be edited after submission.
  8. Submit before the stated deadline. Late applications are not accepted.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Valid CNIC
  • Educational certificates and transcripts (matric onward, as relevant to the post)
  • Domicile certificate
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Updated CV/resume
  • Experience certificates, if applying for a position requiring prior experience

What Happens After You Apply

For most technical and officer-grade positions, shortlisted candidates are called for a written test, followed by an interview. Entry-level contractual roles sometimes move directly to document verification and an interview, skipping a written test. Only shortlisted candidates are contacted — NADRA does not typically respond individually to every applicant.

Watch out for fraud: If anyone contacts you claiming to guarantee a NADRA job or test slot in exchange for payment, this is not an official process. Report such approaches and apply only through careers.nadra.gov.pk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I apply for NADRA jobs?

All NADRA jobs are advertised and applied for exclusively through the official careers portal at careers.nadra.gov.pk. NADRA does not accept applications by email, WhatsApp, or through third-party agents.

Is there an application fee for NADRA jobs?

NADRA does not charge candidates to apply or sit for a test. Be cautious of anyone asking for payment in exchange for a NADRA job or test slot — this is not an official practice.

What documents do I need to apply for a NADRA job?

You will generally need a valid CNIC, your educational certificates and transcripts, a recent photograph, your domicile certificate, and an updated CV or resume, all ready to upload as scanned copies when filling the online form.

What is the typical NADRA recruitment process?

After applying online, shortlisted candidates are typically called for a written test, followed by an interview for most technical and officer-grade positions. Entry-level contractual roles may skip directly to an interview or document verification stage.

Are NADRA jobs permanent or contractual?

NADRA hires for both. Many entry-level and support roles are offered on a contractual basis (often with an initial probation period), while technical, managerial, and HQ positions may be regular or project-based depending on the specific advertisement.

How do I know about new NADRA job openings?

Check careers.nadra.gov.pk directly and regularly, since this is the only official source. Vacancies are also published in major national newspapers at the time of announcement.
