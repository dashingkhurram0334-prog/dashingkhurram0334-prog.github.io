POC Card Fees Pakistan 2026 — How to Apply, Benefits & Visa Exemptions

FEES · VISA EXEMPTIONS · BANKING BENEFITS · NADRA CENTERS · AUGUST 2026

The Pakistan Origin Card, commonly known as POC, is a special identity document issued by NADRA for overseas Pakistanis and persons of Pakistani descent living abroad. This guide explains everything you need to know about applying for a POC, including the latest fee structure, processing times, visa exemptions, banking advantages, and property rights in Pakistan. Whether you're considering applying for a POC or want to understand how it can benefit your life as an overseas Pakistani, this comprehensive guide covers all the essential information in one place.

What Exactly Is the Pakistan Origin Card?

The Pakistan Origin Card is an official travel and identity document specifically designed for overseas Pakistanis. Unlike a regular passport which is for Pakistani nationals only, the POC serves a different purpose. It's a recognition of your Pakistani heritage and connection to Pakistan, whether you hold Pakistani citizenship, have renounced it, or are a foreign national of Pakistani descent. The card functions as an identity document within Pakistan and allows visa-free travel to numerous countries. Most importantly, the POC opens doors to property ownership, business operations, banking services, and government benefits that would otherwise be restricted for foreign nationals in Pakistan. The card is valid for five years and features modern biometric security elements to prevent forgery and misuse. Think of it as a bridge document that maintains your Pakistani connection while you live and work abroad.

Who Can Apply for a Pakistan Origin Card?

Several categories of people qualify for a POC. If you're a Pakistani national living abroad, you're eligible regardless of how long you've been outside Pakistan. Foreign nationals who can prove Pakistani ancestry, including second and third-generation overseas Pakistanis holding foreign passports, are also eligible if they have proper family documentation. People who previously held Pakistani citizenship but have since renounced it or adopted another nationality can apply. Spouses of Pakistani nationals, whether Pakistani or foreign, may qualify under certain conditions. Generally, applicants must be at least 18 years old, though exceptions exist for younger applicants when a guardian applies on their behalf. The key requirement is having valid documentation proving your Pakistani nationality or descent, such as a current or expired Pakistani CNIC, birth certificate from Pakistan, Pakistani passport, or family heritage records.

Why Should You Get a Pakistan Origin Card?

A POC provides tangible, practical benefits that make life easier for overseas Pakistanis. The most obvious advantage is visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to numerous countries, eliminating expensive visa application fees and reducing processing times. For frequent travelers between Pakistan and abroad, this saves substantial time and money.

The property ownership benefit is particularly valuable. POC holders can purchase, own, lease, and sell immovable property in Pakistan without needing special government permission. This is a right strictly limited for foreign nationals, making the POC essential if you want to invest in Pakistani real estate, purchase land, or buy a home in your hometown. You can inherit property, transfer it to family members, and conduct all property transactions as smoothly as Pakistani nationals do.

Banking and financial services become significantly simpler with a POC. Pakistani banks readily open accounts for POC holders without requiring additional documentation or NOC from Pakistani diplomatic missions. You can access foreign exchange services, transfer money, obtain loans for property or business, invest in Pakistani securities, and conduct all banking operations with minimal bureaucratic hassle. This opens opportunities for business investment, real estate financing, and financial portfolio management in Pakistan.

Employment and business opportunities improve considerably. Many jobs in Pakistan officially prefer or require Pakistani nationals or POC holders over foreign nationals. If you're considering business operations in Pakistan, establishing a company is significantly easier with a POC. Government contracts and tenders often give preference to POC holders over foreign nationals.

Educational benefits exist as well. POC holders can enroll in Pakistani educational institutions with simplified admission processes and sometimes at better fee structures than foreign students. Some scholarships are specifically reserved for persons of Pakistani origin.

Visa-Free Countries and Travel Benefits

One of the most appealing benefits of the POC is the ability to travel to numerous countries without obtaining a visa beforehand. POC holders enjoy visa-free entry to most Middle Eastern countries including Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, and Kuwait. Countries like Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and the Philippines offer visa-free access. Turkey, Iran, and numerous African nations are on the list. Additionally, POC holders can enter Pakistan without any visa at all, using only the POC as their travel document at Pakistani immigration counters. For visa-on-arrival countries like the United States (under specific conditions), United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand, POC holders often face streamlined processes compared to foreign nationals. The complete list of visa-free and visa-on-arrival destinations changes periodically as Pakistan negotiates agreements with different countries, so always verify current requirements before traveling. The practical result is that overseas Pakistanis spend less money on visas, wait less time for approvals, and enjoy greater travel freedom across a significant portion of the world.

Banking and Financial Advantages Explained

For overseas Pakistanis managing finances, investments, or business interests in Pakistan, the POC provides substantial banking advantages. Opening a bank account in Pakistan becomes straightforward; most major Pakistani banks accept the POC as primary identification without requiring additional residency certificates or letters from Pakistani embassies. This matters because foreign nationals typically need embassy documentation to open accounts, a process that can take weeks.

Access to foreign exchange and remittance services improves significantly. If you're sending money to family in Pakistan regularly, POC holders often receive better exchange rates and faster processing. Investment opportunities become available; you can invest in Pakistani mutual funds, stock market shares, and other securities with fewer restrictions. Property financing is more readily accessible; banks cheerfully provide mortgages and property loans to POC holders for purchasing homes or commercial property in Pakistan.

Government social security programs and pension schemes become accessible to POC holders in ways they aren't for foreign nationals. If you're planning retirement in Pakistan or want to participate in national saving schemes and investment programs, the POC facilitates participation.

How to Apply for a POC — Step by Step

Applying for a Pakistan Origin Card involves a straightforward process that begins with confirming your eligibility. Determine whether you qualify as a Pakistani national abroad, foreign national of Pakistani descent, or person of Pakistani origin. Gather your essential documents first: valid passport (Pakistani or foreign), proof of Pakistani connection (CNIC, birth certificate, or heritage records), two recent passport-sized photographs, and proof of your current foreign address.

Next, decide whether to apply through a Pakistani embassy or consulate in your country, or through a NADRA center if you're currently in Pakistan. If you're living abroad, contact your nearest Pakistani diplomatic mission. If you're in Pakistan, visit any NADRA center. Both options process POC applications effectively; the choice simply depends on your current location.

Once you've chosen your application method, complete the official POC application form with complete accuracy. Include your full name, date of birth, current address, contact information, employment details, and reason for applying. Provide clear, legible handwriting and sign all required sections. Forms are available at embassies, consulates, or NADRA centers, or can be downloaded from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs website.

Submit your completed application along with all supporting documents and photographs to the relevant office. Staff will review your application for completeness and may ask clarifying questions about your background or documentation. Make sure all documents are original or properly certified; photocopies alone usually aren't accepted for initial applications.

Pay the appropriate processing fee based on your chosen processing speed: Rs. 41,775 ($150) for Normal processing, Rs. 55,700 ($200) for Urgent processing, or Rs. 69,625 ($250) for Executive priority processing. Most centers accept credit cards, bank transfers, or cash payments. Keep your receipt carefully as it contains your application reference number and estimated completion date — you'll need this when collecting your card.

Attend your scheduled appointment for biometric data collection, which includes fingerprints and iris scan, along with a brief verification interview. The entire process takes about 15 minutes. Bring your original documents and receipt. The staff member will verify your identity and may ask questions about your application to ensure everything is in order.

Finally, return to the office on or after your completion date to collect your new POC. Before leaving the office, verify that all details printed on the card are correct — your name, date of birth, card number, and validity dates. Your POC is now valid for five years and is ready for use in travel, banking, property transactions, and all other purposes that require Pakistani identity documentation.

Common Questions About POC

Q: Can I use a POC instead of a passport?
A: The POC works for entering Pakistan and traveling to visa-free countries. For most international travel outside designated countries, you need your national passport. Always verify specific destination requirements before traveling.
Q: How long does POC processing actually take?
A: Processing takes 31 days (Normal), 23 days (Urgent), or 9 days (Executive). These are working days, not calendar days, so actual time depends on weekends and holidays.
Q: Can a foreign national of Pakistani descent get a POC?
A: Yes, absolutely. If you hold a foreign passport but have Pakistani ancestry, you can apply by providing proof of Pakistani heritage through family documents.
Q: Is POC accepted for bank account opening in Pakistan?
A: Yes, most Pakistani banks accept POC as primary identification. Opening an account is much easier for POC holders than for foreign nationals.
Q: Can I buy property with a POC?
A: Yes, one of the main benefits is unrestricted property ownership. POC holders can purchase, own, and sell property without special government permission.
Q: What happens when my POC expires?
A: You can renew it using the same process and current fees. The renewal application is processed like a new application.
