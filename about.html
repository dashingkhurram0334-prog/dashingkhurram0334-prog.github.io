About NADRA Fee Guide

NADRA Fee Guide was built to solve one simple frustration: finding accurate, up-to-date NADRA fee information without digging through outdated forum posts or conflicting search results.

This site brings together the current fee structure for Smart CNIC, B-Form, Family Registration Certificate, NICOP, and related NADRA services — along with practical guides for tracking applications and preparing documents.

We are an independent, unofficial resource. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to NADRA (National Database and Registration Authority) in any way. For official transactions, always use NADRA's own portal at id.nadra.gov.pk or visit a NADRA Registration Center in person.

Fee figures are checked periodically against multiple public sources, but government fee structures can change without notice — always verify the exact amount before making a payment.
