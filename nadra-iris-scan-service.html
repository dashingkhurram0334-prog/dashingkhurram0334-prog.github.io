NADRA Free Iris Scan Registration Service 2026

FREE SERVICE · CHILDREN AGE 3+ · BIOMETRIC SECURITY · JULY 2026

NADRA (National Database and Registration Authority) offers a completely free iris scan registration service for children aged three years and above. This biometric service strengthens identity verification records from an early age, creating a secure digital identity foundation for Pakistan's children. The iris scan is a non-invasive, quick procedure that provides lifelong biometric identification. This comprehensive guide explains what the service is, who is eligible, why it matters, the complete registration process, and answers to frequently asked questions about NADRA's iris registration initiative.

What Is NADRA Iris Scan Registration Service?

The NADRA Iris Scan Registration Service is a free biometric enrollment program that captures and stores a child's iris (the colored part of the eye) data in NADRA's secure national database. The iris is unique to every individual—more distinctive than fingerprints—making it an extremely reliable form of identification. NADRA's iris scanning technology creates a digital record of this biometric identifier, establishing a permanent identity profile that can be used for verification purposes throughout the child's life. This service is part of NADRA's broader initiative to build comprehensive biometric records for Pakistan's population, starting from childhood. The entire process is non-invasive, painless, and requires no needles or injections—the child simply looks into an iris scanning device for a few seconds while the biometric data is captured electronically.

Who Is Eligible for NADRA Iris Scan Registration?

Any child aged three years and above is eligible for the free iris scan registration service. There is no upper age limit; even adults and senior citizens of any age can register if they haven't already. The service is open to all age CNIC holders — whether someone is 5 years old, 25 years old, 50 years old, or 80 years old, they can come to NADRA and register their iris biometric data completely free of charge. Pakistani nationals and residents are eligible. The service is specifically designed to encourage enrollment across all age groups to establish comprehensive biometric identity records for Pakistan's entire population. Guardians or parents can bring children to any NADRA Registration Center for enrollment. Adults can come independently. Children with visual impairments should contact their nearest NADRA center to inquire about alternative biometric options, as the iris scan requires the ability to look into the scanning device.

Why Is Iris Scan Registration Important?

Biometric identification using iris scans provides several critical advantages. First, it creates a unique, permanent identity record that cannot be forged, stolen, or duplicated—unlike documents which can be lost or damaged. Second, it strengthens Pakistan's national database, improving government services and security. Third, as children grow older and interact with banks, educational institutions, travel authorities, and government agencies, having an established iris biometric record accelerates identity verification processes. Fourth, it protects children against identity fraud and misuse. Finally, iris-based identification is increasingly used globally for border control, banking access, and official documentation, so establishing this record early prepares children for a more secure digital future.

The NADRA Iris Scan Process

1

Visit Your Nearest NADRA Center

Go to any NADRA Registration Center with the child during operating hours (typically 9 AM to 4 PM, Monday to Friday). No appointment is usually required for iris scans, though calling ahead to confirm availability is recommended. Bring the child's existing identity document (B-Form for children under 18, or CNIC if the child has one).

2

Provide Basic Information

At the intake counter, inform staff that you are requesting the free iris scan registration service. Staff will collect basic information: child's name, date of birth, guardian's name, and contact details. This information is already in NADRA's system if the child has a B-Form or CNIC, so the process is quick.

3

Iris Scan Procedure

The child is guided to the iris scanning device. The process is completely painless and takes less than one minute. The child simply looks into the device, and the biometric scanner captures the iris pattern. Most children find it a quick and painless experience. Very young children (under 5) may need gentle guidance or encouragement, and NADRA staff are experienced in working with children.

4

Data Storage and Confirmation

Once the iris scan is captured, the biometric data is immediately encrypted and stored in NADRA's national database. The guardian receives a receipt or confirmation slip acknowledging that the iris scan has been registered. The process is complete, and the child now has an established iris biometric record in NADRA's system.

Benefits of NADRA Iris Registration

✓ Unique Identification: Iris patterns are more distinctive than fingerprints and cannot be altered, providing lifelong, unchangeable identity verification.
✓ Security Protection: Early biometric registration protects children against identity fraud and misuse throughout their lives.
✓ Faster Official Processes: Future applications for school enrollment, bank accounts, passports, or government services will process faster with established biometric records.
✓ Completely Free: The entire iris scan registration service is at no cost to families, removing financial barriers to biometric enrollment.
✓ Quick and Painless: The iris scan takes less than a minute and causes no discomfort to the child.
✓ National Database Strengthening: Widespread biometric enrollment improves Pakistan's national security and government service delivery infrastructure.

Required Documents

✓

Child's Identity Document: B-Form (Child Registration Certificate) or CNIC if the child already has one. If the child has neither, bring the birth certificate and guardian's CNIC.

✓

Guardian's CNIC: Original CNIC of the parent or legal guardian accompanying the child (for verification purposes).

✓

Birth Certificate: If the child does not yet have a B-Form, bring the birth certificate from the Union Council.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can adults register for iris scan, or is it only for children?
A: Both children and adults can register. The service is free for all age CNIC holders — whether you are 5 years old, 25 years old, 50 years old, or older, you can visit any NADRA center and register your iris biometric data completely free. There is no age limit for this service.
Q: Is the iris scan service really free?
A: Yes, completely free. NADRA offers this service at no cost as part of its national biometric registration initiative. There are no hidden charges.
Q: Is the procedure safe for young children?
A: Yes, completely safe. The iris scan is non-invasive, painless, and uses only light to capture the iris pattern. No contact with the eye is made.
Q: How long does the iris scan take?
A: Less than one minute from start to finish. The actual scan takes only seconds.
Q: Can I register my child if they wear glasses?
A: Yes. Glasses do not interfere with iris scanning. The child can keep glasses on during the procedure.
Q: What if my child has a visual impairment?
A: Contact your nearest NADRA center to discuss available options. NADRA staff can advise on alternative biometric registration methods if iris scanning is not suitable.
Q: Do I need an appointment for iris scan registration?
A: Not typically. Walk-ins are usually accepted, but calling your local NADRA center beforehand to confirm that iris scanning services are available that day is a good practice.
Q: Will my child's iris data be secure?
A: Yes. NADRA uses advanced encryption and security protocols to protect all biometric data stored in its national database. The data is accessible only to authorized government agencies for identity verification purposes.
