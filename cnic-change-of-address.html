How to Change Address on CNIC

MODIFICATION GUIDE · UPDATED JULY 2026

Moved to a new city or locality? Your CNIC address should match your current residence, since it's used for verification in banking, SIM registration, property, and other official matters. Updating it is a straightforward "Modification" request at NADRA — here's exactly how it works.

When Should You Update Your Address?

✓

You've permanently relocated to a new city, town, or neighborhood.

✓

Your current CNIC shows your parents' or a previous address that no longer applies.

✓

You need an updated address for a bank account, visa application, or property registration.

✓

You've moved abroad and need your NICOP or CNIC to reflect your overseas address.

Documents Required

✓

Original CNIC: Your current, existing Smart CNIC (or its number if the card is lost).

✓

Proof of New Address: A recent utility bill (electricity, gas, or water) dated within the last 3 months, OR a rent agreement, OR a property ownership document.

✓

Domicile or Residency Proof: Required in some cases if moving between provinces — check with your local center.

✓

Passport-sized Photograph: One recent colored photo with a white background, in case biometric re-verification is needed.

Step-by-Step Process

1

Visit a NADRA Center or Use Pak-ID App

Address changes can be done in person at any NADRA Registration Center, or online through the Pak-Identity portal / Pak-ID mobile app if biometric re-verification isn't required.

2

Submit a Modification Request

Request a "Modification" (not a fresh application) form, and select "Address" as the field you want updated. Attach your proof-of-address document.

3

Biometric Verification

In most cases you'll need to reverify your fingerprints at the center, even for an address-only change, since NADRA re-issues the physical card.

4

Pay the Modification Fee

Pay the applicable fee based on your chosen processing speed — Normal, Urgent, or Executive — at the counter or online via the portal.

5

Collect Your Updated CNIC

A new physical card with the updated address will be issued and can be collected once ready, following the same tracking and collection process as a fresh CNIC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I update my address without visiting a NADRA center?
In some cases, yes — if your biometrics don't need re-verification, you can submit an address modification request through the Pak-Identity portal or Pak-ID app. Otherwise, an in-person visit is required.
Do I get a new CNIC number after an address change?
No, your 13-digit CNIC number stays the same for life. Only the printed address on the card is updated, and a new physical card is reissued.
Is proof of address mandatory, or can I self-declare?
Proof of address (a utility bill, rent agreement, or ownership document) is required — NADRA does not accept a self-declaration alone for address modifications.
Can a wife update her address to match her husband's CNIC address?
Yes, a wife can update her address to match her husband's address on record, typically by submitting the modification request along with her husband's CNIC copy and marriage certificate as supporting documents.
Important: Keep your CNIC address current — an outdated address can cause delays in banking KYC, SIM verification, and other services that cross-check with NADRA records. Always confirm the latest fee and process at id.nadra.gov.pk or your nearest NADRA Registration Center before applying.
