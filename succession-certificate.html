NADRA Succession Certificate

GUIDE & FEES · 2026

A Succession Certificate is an official legal document issued by NADRA that allows the lawful heirs of a deceased person to claim, transfer, or manage the deceased's assets — including bank accounts, shares, property, insurance, and other movable/immovable assets — without going to court.

Fee Structure

Fees are based on the total value of the inherited estate:

Asset ValueFee (PKR)Notes
Assets worth more than Rs. 1,00,000Rs. 20,000+ Rs. 2,000 extra in some provinces (e.g. Sindh)
Assets worth less than Rs. 1,00,000Rs. 10,000Standard rate
Duplicate certificateRs. 5,000If original is lost/damaged

Note for overseas Pakistanis: Fees are paid in PKR after currency conversion at the prevailing rate on the date of payment. No separate overseas rate applies.

Documents Required

✓

Computerized Death Certificate of the deceased (from Union Council)

✓

Cancelled CNIC copy of the deceased

✓

Valid CNICs / SNICs / NICOPs of all legal heirs

✓

Family Registration Certificate (FRC) — fresh NADRA-issued, showing complete lineage

✓

Asset details (bank account numbers, property documents, shares, insurance papers, etc.)

✓

Biometric fingerprints of all legal heirs (can be done via Pak-ID app for overseas heirs)

Application Process — Step by Step

1

Visit NADRA Succession Facilitation Unit (SFU)

Go to your nearest NADRA Registration Center or dedicated Succession Facilitation Unit. Overseas heirs can initiate via the Pak-ID portal or mobile app.

2

Submit Documents & Asset Details

Provide all required documents listed above, along with details of every asset being claimed.

3

Biometric Verification of All Heirs

All legal heirs must provide fingerprints. Overseas heirs can complete biometrics via the Pak-ID app or at a Pakistani Consulate — no need to travel to Pakistan.

4

Public Notice (14 Days)

NADRA publishes a notice in a national newspaper for 14 days. This allows any unlisted heirs or creditors to raise objections.

5

Certificate Issued

If no objections are raised within 14 days, the Succession Certificate or Letter of Administration is printed and issued to the legal heirs.
